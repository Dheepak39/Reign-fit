How your score is calculated

Score = PR + Consistency

Two things matter: your best performance and how often you show up.

💪
PR Score

Your best session ever, per exercise.
Push-up: 1 pt / rep
Squat: 1 pt / rep
Knee push-up: 1 pt / 2 reps
Plank: 1 pt / 2 s

📅
Consistency Bonus

+10 points for each day you train this month.

Max 3 sessions per exercise per day counted.

Monthly Score = PR Score + Consistency Points  ·  Resets each month
